This is the blog site for [gojimmypi](/author/gojimmypi/), moved to github after several years at [https://gojimmypi.blogspot.com/](https://gojimmypi.blogspot.com/).

There's only one [gojimmypi.com](https://gojimmypi.com) domain that just forwards to [gojimmypi.github.io](https://gojimmypi.github.io).

I'm passionate about Educational Open Source and the Right to Repair.

After many years in the technology industry, I have repeatedly seen companies come and go. The aftermath:

- Hardware bricks. 
- Landfills full of toxic chemicals and potentially valuable rare minerals.
- Proprietary software goes without security updates.
- Applications are no longer compatible with operating system updates.
- Business suffers.

As the years go by the rate at which this occurs only increases, leaving customers to rebuild again and again. 
This is an epic waste of time and money... and a story that has been repeated an embarrassingly large number of times: 
A major badge of dishonor that we all bear.

We can do better.
